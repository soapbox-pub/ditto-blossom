    The buttons

    • ✓
      Looks fine marks it checked. You won't see it in Review again.
    • ✕
      Not allowed or Ban deletes it, and stops it being uploaded again. You get a few seconds to undo.
    • −
      Remove just deletes it. It can be uploaded again.
    • ↻
      Purge, top right, appears when admin.cdn names a cache in front of this server, like Cloudflare: it may keep serving what you delete for up to a year, until you purge it there.
    • !
      Child abuse is only for child sexual abuse material. See below.

    In Review, keys work too: 1 looks fine, 2 not allowed, space skip, U undo.

    If you see child abuse

    1. Press Child abuse. It's hidden from everyone, the uploader is banned, and a locked copy of it and of all they uploaded is kept for the report. Don't use Ban or Remove: they delete the copy.
    2. Report it to NCMEC, the same day if you can. Evidence has what to copy into the form.
    3. Enter the report number in Evidence. The copy is deleted a year later.
    Do this once, now: register with NCMEC as a service provider. It's free and takes a few days.

    More detail

    US law asks you to report child sexual abuse material you know of to NCMEC, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, and to keep a copy for a year after (18 U.S.C. § 2258A). Outside the US, check your own country's rules.

    Not registered yet? Report it anyway, on the public form or at 1-800-843-5678, and say you've kept a copy.

    Don't download it, look at it again, or show it to anyone but NCMEC or the police. If the police ask you to destroy it, delete its files from the evidence folder.

    If a CDN sits in front of this server, purge the file there too: the Purge button, top right, lists what to purge. If your web server keeps logs, keep its lines for the file.
